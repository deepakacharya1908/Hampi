Massive monolithic Ganesha statues carved from single granite boulders.

Krishna Temple & Bazaar

Stunning temple complex with an extensive, partially excavated ancient market street.

Ugra Narasimha
Afternoon

The largest monolithic statue in Hampi, depicting the fierce avatar of Vishnu.

6
Badavilinga
Afternoon

A giant Shiva Linga perpetually surrounded by water in a stone chamber.

7
Kamalapura Museum & Sunset point
Evening

ASI museum containing recovered artifacts, concluding with a serene sunset view.

Afternoon

A grand, domed building that once housed the imperial war elephants of the empire.

Vijaya Vittala Temple
